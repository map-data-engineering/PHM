Site Check

Before accrediting a new outlet, check how many existing CPPs already sit within walking distance of the proposed location.

How to use it. Drop a pin on the map at the proposed pharmacy location (or paste GPS coordinates / search an address). PharmaScope returns the number of accredited outlets within your chosen radius, the distance to the nearest one, the density per km², and a suggested verdict: saturated, well-served, adequate, or under-served. Use it as evidence, not a decision.
Drop a pin on the map to check this location.
Verdict thresholds are density-based (accredited outlets per km² inside the search radius). They are guidance only: the Pharmacy Council retains full discretion on new applications.
