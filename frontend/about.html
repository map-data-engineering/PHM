About PharmaScope

Pharmacy access intelligence for Tanzania, built directly on Pharmacy Council and TMDA accreditation records.

Mission

PharmaScope makes access to accredited drug outlets measurable, comparable, and continually updated across Tanzania — so the Pharmacy Council, TMDA, implementers, and researchers can plan around who can reach a legitimate outlet, not just where outlets appear on a map.

Regulator-sourced

Primary data comes directly from the Pharmacy Council of Tanzania and TMDA accreditation records — never from second-hand aggregators.

Continually updated

The registry is refreshed as new accreditation exports arrive, so figures like outlet and region counts stay current rather than fixed at build time.

Reproducible

Every outlet record passes through a documented, rerunnable import pipeline — column standardisation, coordinate validation, and a stable outlet ID that survives re-imports.

Population-first

Each tool is built around the people an outlet is meant to serve: finding one nearby, checking whether a new one may open, and tracking gaps in coverage.

What's inside

Drug Outlet Registry

Search, filter, and map every accredited outlet as points or admin-boundary choropleths. Requires sign-in.

Find Pharmacy

Public tool: enter an address or share your location to find the nearest accredited drug outlets, with directions.

Site Check

Regulator tool: checks a proposed premises against the Pharmacy Council's official siting rules — distance from existing pharmacies, public health facilities, and documented exemptions.

Data Quality

Regulator/Data Team tool: tracks completeness and provenance of the underlying registry — geocoding rate, missing fields, and admin-boundary match rate.
