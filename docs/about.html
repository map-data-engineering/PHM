About PharmaScope

Pharmacy access intelligence beyond static outlet inventories.

Mission

Our mission is to make access to accredited medicines outlets measurable, comparable, and continually updated across Tanzania — so that the Pharmacy Council, TFDA, implementers, and researchers plan around who can reach a legitimate outlet, not just where outlets appear on a map.

We commit to four principles:

  • Open — every dataset and method behind the portal is freely available under permissive licences, without registration.
  • Regulator-sourced — primary data comes directly from the Pharmacy Council of Tanzania and TFDA accreditation records, never from second-hand aggregators.
  • Reproducible — the ETL pipeline that produces every table, chart, and map on this site is versioned, documented, and rerunnable from source.
  • Population-first — each layer of analysis is built around the people an outlet is meant to serve, not just the outlet itself.

The Gap We Fill

Existing pharmacy datasets tend to list outlets without saying whether populations can realistically reach them or what medicines they actually stock. PharmaScope goes further by providing:

  • Population-demand modelling — reachability analysis beyond simple location pins
  • Service-level intelligence — mapping of which malaria commodities, antimicrobials, and priority medicines are stocked where
  • Access metrics at scale — outlets per 10,000 population, equity of coverage, and gaps by region and ward

PharmaScope addresses these gaps as an open, reproducible data platform for Tanzania’s private drug-retail sector.

Intelligence Layers

1. CPP Registry Lake

Standardized outlet data from the Pharmacy Council of Tanzania and TFDA. Harmonized schema; direct regulator sourcing.

2. Demand-Side Access

Population catchment zones using road networks; travel-time isochrones showing not just outlet location, but reachability.

3. Service Gap Intelligence

Which medicines and priority services exist where. Malaria commodities, antimicrobials, and outlet-level service profiles.

4. General Information

Region-level descriptive statistics — outlet counts, business type, accreditation source — that give a fast picture of the registry without claiming reachability we have not yet modelled.

ETL Pipeline

The PharmaScope ETL pipeline ingests the Pharmacy Council and TFDA records through three steps:

  1. Extract — pull the latest CPP inventory export (Pharmacy Council of Tanzania) into data-raw/.
  2. Transform — standardise columns to a shared schema, normalise region/district casing, coalesce the two coordinate sources (tablet GPS and hand GPS), validate coordinates against Tanzania’s bounding box, and mint a stable outlet UID.
  3. Load — publish a versioned CSV (data/tanzania/addo_standardized.csv) that the portal reads directly at page load.
